# Code of Conduct — PolyGuard

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders of the **PolyGuard** project (TeamGamma, QuantumLogicsLabs) pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

### ✅ Positive Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and graciously accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the team and the project
- Showing empathy towards other team members
- Helping newer contributors understand the codebase
- Acknowledging the work and contributions of others
- Keeping discussions focused on technical merit

### ❌ Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment of any team member
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Taking credit for someone else's work or contribution
- Deliberately sabotaging another team member's work
- Dismissing or belittling contributions without constructive feedback
- Any conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

---

## Team-Specific Guidelines

Since PolyGuard is a university project with a deadline, these additional guidelines apply:

### Communication

- Respond to team messages within **24 hours** on working days
- Use clear and respectful language in all team communications
- If you are blocked or unable to complete your task, inform the team captain **immediately**
- Do not make promises you cannot keep regarding deadlines

### Code Contributions

- Never commit directly to the `main` branch without review
- Do not delete or overwrite another team member's work without discussion
- Always test your code before submitting a pull request
- Document your changes clearly in commit messages
- Credit your teammates when building on their work

### Model and Dataset Work

- Do not upload untested models to the shared Google Drive without informing the team
- Always keep a backup before overwriting datasets
- Share training results with the full team, not just the team captain
- Never fabricate or exaggerate model performance metrics

### Meetings and Deadlines

- Attend all scheduled team meetings or inform in advance if you cannot
- Complete your assigned tasks by the agreed deadline
- The **June 6 deadline** is non-negotiable — prioritize accordingly
- Raise blockers early — do not wait until the deadline

## Enforcement

### Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to:

- **Team Captain:** Maryam Fareed (direct message)
- **GitHub Issues:** Open a private issue labeled `conduct`

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The privacy and security of the reporter will be respected.

### Consequences

Team members who violate this Code of Conduct may face:

**1. Warning**
A private written warning explaining the violation and expected behavior going forward.

**2. Temporary Restriction**
Temporary removal from team communications or code review responsibilities.

**3. Permanent Removal**
In severe cases, permanent removal from the project and reporting to university authorities if necessary.
